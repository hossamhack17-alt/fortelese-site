    <h3>Is an Online MBA in Finance respected by Wall Street?</h3>
    <p><strong>Absolutely.</strong> Top financial institutions and private equity firms do not distinguish between online and on-campus degrees, provided the institution holds <strong>AACSB accreditation</strong>. The prestige lies in the university's brand and the rigor of its quantitative curriculum.</p>
    
    <h3>Does this degree accelerate the CFA charter process?</h3>
    <p><strong>Yes.</strong> Vanguard programs (such as the University of Florida) deliberately map their coursework to the CFA Institute Candidate Body of Knowledge (CBOK), providing a dual advantage of an MBA and robust CFA exam preparation.</p>
    
    <h3>What is the true timeline for a positive Return on Investment (ROI)?</h3>
    <p>With an average base salary jump exceeding 50% post-graduation (as reported by the Financial Times), most alumni recoup their educational investment within <strong>24 to 36 months</strong>, followed by decades of compounded earning power.</p>
