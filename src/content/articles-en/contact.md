## Email

Editorial, corrections and permissions: <a href="mailto:admin1@fortelese.com">admin1@fortelese.com</a>

We read every message and usually reply within two to three working days. Messages about a published article are handled first.

## Reporting an error

Send the page URL, the exact sentence, and what you believe is correct. You do not need a long explanation; precision matters more than style. We review the claim, correct the article, and note the update date on the page.

## What to include

- The page URL if the message concerns an article.
- One or two sentences describing the issue.
- A verifiable source when the request is a factual correction.

## What we do not provide

The site does not give personal medical, legal or financial advice, and does not recommend providers for payment. Advertising enquiries are handled under the editorial policy and never influence article order or conclusions.

## Privacy

We ask for no personal data beyond your email address and your message, and we do not share correspondence with third parties except where the law requires it.


## Disclaimer

This article is general education, not financial advice. Figures and rules differ by provider and change over time; verify current terms in writing.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is this a recommendation?** No. It explains how to compare; it names no provider to buy from.

**Will my numbers match?** No. Results depend on your income, obligations and the provider's current terms.

**Where do I verify?** In the document you sign and with the relevant regulator.
