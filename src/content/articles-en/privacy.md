## What we collect

The site is a static publication. We do not require an account and do not ask for personal details to read an article.

Technical data is processed by the hosting and network layer to deliver pages and protect the service: the requested URL, an approximate location derived from the network, the browser type, and error information. This is used for delivery and security, not for profiling.

## Cookies and similar technologies

The English edition does not set cookies for tracking or advertising. Your language choice and display preferences are stored locally in your browser, on your device, and are not transmitted to us.

If an advertising or measurement service is enabled in the future, this page will name it, state what it sets, and a consent mechanism will appear in the regions where consent is required before it runs.

## Your choices

- Clear local storage from your browser settings to remove saved display preferences.
- Block or delete cookies through your browser; the articles remain readable.
- Use a private window if you prefer not to keep local preferences.

## Third parties

The only third-party request the pages rely on is the network provider that serves them. We do not embed third-party tracking scripts in articles.
