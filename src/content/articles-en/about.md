Fortelese publishes explanatory guides in two separate editions: Arabic on the root of the site, and English under `/en/`. Each edition has its own navigation, metadata, feeds and article set. No article is published in both languages under a single URL.

## What we write about

Three areas, chosen because readers routinely face the same questions in them:

- **Education** — online and part-time degrees, accreditation, what a programme really requires.
- **Money** — budgeting, loans, fees, insurance mechanics, and how to compare offers.
- **Property and everyday decisions** — inspections, subscriptions, cash planning.

## How an article is made

1. The question is chosen from what a reader actually asks, not from a keyword list alone.
2. The structure follows the decision: what it is, what to compare, what to ask, what to avoid.
3. Numeric examples are shown as calculations with the formula, never as claims about a market price.
4. Anything that could be read as personal advice carries an explicit disclaimer about its scope.

## What we do not do

- We do not publish invented statistics, rankings or prices.
- We do not accept payment for a recommendation.
- We do not present medical, legal or investment advice as personal guidance.
- We do not copy other publishers without attribution.

## Corrections

Write to us with the page URL and the exact sentence. We correct the article itself and record the update date on the page. Substantive corrections are not hidden in a comment thread.

## Disclaimer

This article is general education, not financial advice. Figures and rules differ by provider and change over time; verify current terms in writing.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is this a recommendation?** No. It explains how to compare; it names no provider to buy from.

**Will my numbers match?** No. Results depend on your income, obligations and the provider's current terms.

**Where do I verify?** In the document you sign and with the relevant regulator.
